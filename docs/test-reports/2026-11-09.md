# Test pass — 2026-11-09

A full pass over every feature and flow: automated suites, signed-out access,
a brand-new learner from sign-up to every surface, keyboard and pointer, desktop
and 375 px, light and dark, API abuse cases, and the production build.

**Verdict.** Every surface works end to end, and all 23 bugs found here were
fixed and re-verified. The biggest remaining gap is **content**, not code: the
practice bank holds 8 cards, so a learner runs out in one sitting.

Test learner: a UK Year 11 student (stage 10) taking GCSE Mathematics, with an
exam on 2027-05-20 and 20 minutes a day. The credentials are in `.env.local` as
`GRASP_TEST_*_3`.

## Bugs found and fixed

**Content and the model pipeline**

1. **LaTeX backslashes dropped by structured output.** A strict-schema JSON
   decoder only allows valid escapes. As a result `\sqrt`, `\dfrac`, `\sin`,
   `\circ` and `\phi` arrived as `sqrt`, `dfrac`… in 5 of 8 newly verified cards
   (`$sin\theta=-dfrac{3}{5}$`). Fixed in `parseModelJson` via
   `core/llm/latex.ts`, and 15 stored questions were repaired.
2. **Cards citing material the learner never sees** ("According to the
   passage…", "In the worked example…", "the triangle shown"). This affected 9
   of 30 generated cards. It is now a structural fault, and 4 verified cards
   were re-audited to `rejected`.
3. **Bare function names set in italics** (`$sin\theta$` renders as s·i·n·θ).
   Function names are now set upright at render time.
4. **A GCSE learner retrieved nothing.** The corpus is tagged CBSE and the
   curriculum filter was hard. Curriculum is now a preference, with a labelled
   top-up from other syllabuses.

**Security**

5. **`/debug/gateway` made two live model calls per anonymous request in
   production**, even though it returned 404. A layout's `notFound()` does not
   stop its page rendering in parallel. Fixed with `middleware.ts` plus a guard
   inside the page.
6. **No security headers, and `X-Powered-By` was sent.** Added
   `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy` and
   `Permissions-Policy`, and turned `X-Powered-By` off.
7. **`DELETE /api/sessions` reported success for another learner's chat.** It
   changed nothing, because the write is scoped to the learner, but now it
   returns 404. The sidebar keeps a chat in the list if its delete fails.

**Learning loop**

8. **A card was re-served seconds after it was answered**, while its solution
   was still on screen, giving free mastery. Cards answered in the last 12 h
   are now held back.
9. **No end-of-set screen.** The feed looped on the same cards. There is now an
   "All caught up" state, and Today offers Ask instead of practice that isn't
   there.
10. The verdict showed a concept slug
    (`bounds-of-sine-… → 45% learning`). It now shows the concept's name.

**Interface**

11. **Link-styled buttons lost their label on hover.** `a:hover` outranked
    `.btn`, leaving blue text on a blue button. This affected every link-button
    in the app.
12. **Ask showed internal flags** (`UNGROUNDED`, `budget-shed`) to learners. Two
    are now translated into plain language and the rest are hidden.
13. Today said "Welcome back" to a brand-new learner and had no exam countdown.
14. A sign-up with a short password showed the sign-in error.
15. The exam name and date fields had no labels.
16. The login page had no logo or way home.
17. Plot code (Mafs + mathjs) was bundled into every page that shows Markdown.
    It now loads when a plot first appears: Reps first-load JS went from 378 to
    239 kB and Ask from 394 to 273 kB.

**Accessibility**

18. Collapsed sidebar: "New chat" and the account button had no accessible
    name, and the toggle always said "Collapse".
19. The closed phone drawer stayed in the tab order, and focus wasn't moved into
    it or returned when it closed. The background also scrolled behind it.
20. Ochre text had 3.6:1 contrast in the light theme (Slips pills, exam chip).
    Added an `--ochre-ink` text token.
21. Reps had no `h1`, and Slips filters didn't expose their pressed state.
22. The skip-link target wasn't focusable.
23. The account menu used `role="menu"` around a radio group without menu
    keyboard support. It is now a disclosure.

## Not a bug (checked)

- **Onboarding chips, age field and checkbox** are accessible. The 2026-11-08
  finding came from the inspector printing `title` first.
- **A one-off `GET /ask` 500 in dev** was a webpack hot-reload race while files
  were being edited. It did not recur, and production is clean.
- **Ask mode radios** are named "Answer", "Teach" and "Coach"; some inspectors
  show the `value` instead.

## Still open, in priority order

1. **Practice content volume.** Only 9 global chunks and 8 verified cards exist.
   Ingest more material, then run `pnpm cards:build` at scale.
2. **`card.generate@2`.** The generator still writes passage references (now
   rejected, which wastes calls) and single-backslash LaTeX (now repaired). The
   version bump needs a `card.generate` golden set of at least 10 cases first.
3. **Prompt quality from the eval baseline:**
   - `slip.classify` (6/11) over-uses "misconception" where the right label is
     careless or misread.
   - Coach (6/10) asks two questions at once and doesn't explain after two
     misses.
   - Teach sometimes ends on "Does this make sense?".
4. **Answer latency.** `/api/attempt` makes about 7 sequential Mongo round
   trips. Inserting the event first and reading mastery in parallel would bring
   it near 300 ms.
5. **CSP.** It needs a nonce or hashes for the pre-paint theme script, and an
   allowance for KaTeX inline styles.
6. **Playwright end-to-end tests with axe in CI.** This pass was manual; it
   should be repeatable.
7. Smaller items:
   - The "Press 1–4" hint shows on touch phones.
   - Slip notes read stiffly ("so please review…").
   - The Map's selected-node panel can sit below the fold on short screens.
